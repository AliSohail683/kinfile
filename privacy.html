Privacy

Last updated 19 October 2026. This covers the Kinfile website and early-access list. The app will have its own policy before it launches.

What we collect

When you join the early-access list, we store:

  • your email address
  • the answers you choose to give: where you are in the process, your role, and anything you write in the optional note
  • the page or link that sent you to us (for example, a newsletter name), and the time you joined

We don't use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.

How we use it

  • to email you about Kinfile: early access, product updates and occasional questions about what you need
  • to understand what families are dealing with, so we build the right thing first

We never sell or rent your information, and we don't share it with advertisers.

Where it's stored

The list is stored in a database hosted by Supabase in the United States. Only the Kinfile team can read it. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log basic request data such as IP addresses, as any web host does.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from any email, or ask us to delete your information at any time by writing to alisohail6835@gmail.com. We'll confirm when it's done.

Children

The early-access list is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
